# Vault for Android

Vault stores logins and passwords in an encrypted vault on your phone. By Rednave.

## Easiest install and updates

Get App Hub once from https://github.com/thoroftroy/App-Hub/releases, then use it to install and update this app and the rest of the suite. You will not need to download APKs by hand.

## Install

Requires Android 10 or newer (best on Android 13+).

1. Copy the APK file to your phone (USB, Bluetooth, cloud drive — whatever you like) and tap it.
2. Allow "install from unknown sources" when asked.
3. Open the app from your app drawer.

## Using Vault

- Create a vault with a master password (12+ characters). Everything is encrypted before it reaches storage.
- Save entries with titles, usernames and passwords. The editor shows a password-strength meter and can generate strong passwords.
- Usernames and passwords copy with Android's sensitive-clipboard marking, and the clipboard is cleared automatically after a short time.
- Lock any time; the vault also locks when the app goes to the background or after five minutes idle.
- Backups are encrypted files you can export and re-import on an empty vault. Keep your master password somewhere safe.

There is no password recovery, sync, autofill or fingerprint unlock, and the app has not had an independent security audit.

## Look and feel

Open About inside the app to choose between Aqua, Bliss, Dark Bliss and Super, and to adjust motion. Aqua is the default.
